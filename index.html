About Us

"One holder, every drink."

At Xpandi, we believe your drive should be as comfortable and stylish as the destination. We design innovative, adaptable cup holder solutions that fit any vehicle and any cup — from a morning espresso to an afternoon smoothie.

Our accessories are crafted with precision and purpose, celebrating functional artistry that elevates the everyday driving experience.

Our Product

The Xpandi Cup Holder Expander

Our flagship product is a universal cup holder expander designed to fit virtually any vehicle's existing cup holder. Its expandable design accommodates bottles, tumblers, standard cups, and oversized drinks with ease.

  • Fits cups from 2" to 4.5" diameter
  • Non-slip base keeps your drink secure
  • Sleek, minimal design that matches any interior
  • Durable, premium-grade materials
